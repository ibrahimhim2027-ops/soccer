About

The Player

I'm Ibrahim Mahmoud, a class of 2027 fullback and winger from Newburgh, Indiana. I wear #1 for LouCity Academy, and I'm comfortable on either flank, whether that's defending as a fullback or attacking as a winger.

My game is built on the overlap. I want to be the outlet when we're building, get to the endline, and put a ball into the box. Coaches usually mention my speed and agility first, along with the pace to get back and recover when we lose it. On the ball I'm confident playing accurate passes, threading through balls, and shooting when the lane opens.

Off the field I attend Signature School in Evansville. According to U.S. News & World Report, it is the number one charter school in the nation, its third time since 2022, and the number two high school in the nation for the fourth year running. I carry a 4.0 GPA there and plan to study mechanical engineering. My goal is to play college soccer while earning a degree I can build a career on.
